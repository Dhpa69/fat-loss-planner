How this is calculated

Katch-McArdle (used when body fat % is known): BMR = 370 + 21.6 × LBM(kg), where LBM = weight × (1 − BF%). It's more accurate for people with more or less muscle than average.

Mifflin-St Jeor (when body fat is unknown): 10×kg + 6.25×cm − 5×age + 5 (men) or − 161 (women).

TDEE = BMR × activity multiplier. Deficit = weekly loss (lb) × 3,500 kcal ÷ 7.

Floor: calories never go below 1,500 (men) / 1,200 (women), or your BMR if that's higher. If the floor applies, you'll lose more slowly than the slider says, and the app shows the real rate.

How this is calculated

Assuming lean mass is preserved (with high protein + resistance training), target weight = LBM ÷ (1 − goal BF%). Everything you lose to get there is counted as fat: fat to lose = current weight − target weight.

If you haven't entered body fat %, it's estimated from BMI with the Deurenberg formula (1.2×BMI + 0.23×age − 10.8×(male) − 5.4) for the goal only. That estimate can be off by ±5 points, so measuring helps.

Timeline: simulated week by week at your chosen % of the current weight, which shrinks as you get lighter, capped at 2 lb/week. If the calorie floor or an override is active, it uses the rate those calories imply instead.

How this is calculated

Protein: 1.0 g per lb of goal weight (the target weight from your goal BF%, if available), or 1.0–1.2 g per lb of lean mass. It helps keep muscle during a deficit. 4 kcal/g.

Fat: 0.3–0.4 g per lb of goal weight, raised if needed so it's at least 20% of calories (to support hormones). 9 kcal/g.

Carbs: whatever calories remain, at 4 kcal/g. Fiber: 14 g per 1,000 kcal, at least 38 g (men) / 25 g (women).

How adaptive adjustment works

Once your log covers at least 14 days, the app fits a straight trend line (least squares) through the last 4 weeks of weigh-ins, which smooths out day-to-day water swings. If the actual weekly loss is under 75% of what your calorie target should produce, it estimates your real maintenance as intake + actual loss × 3,500 ÷ 7 and suggests a new target that keeps the deficit you chose (never below the floor). This assumes you actually ate the target. If you didn't, tighten your tracking before you cut calories.

    How minutes and kcal are estimated

    Minutes = 5-minute warm-up + Σ sets × (≈40 s of work, or the timed duration, + rest). Cardio blocks use their stated duration.

    kcal = MET × body weight (kg) × hours, using your latest weight from the planner. MET values are typical Compendium of Physical Activities figures; actual burn varies ±20–30%. Don't eat it back one-for-one.
