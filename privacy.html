Privacy Policy — Fat-Loss Planner

Last updated: October 21, 2026

On your device. Your profile, food log, weight log, workouts and settings are stored on your device. Without an account nothing leaves your device, except food searches and barcode numbers sent to Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central to look up nutrition.

Optional account & cloud sync (Pro). If you sign in (Sign in with Apple or email), a copy of your app data is stored in our database (Supabase) so you can use it on several devices. Only you can read it (row-level security). We also store the date your free trial started.

Apple Health. If you allow it, the app reads weight, steps, workouts and active energy to show in the app. Health data is not used for advertising, not sold and not shared, and is not included in Coach/Doctor links.

Coach / Doctor (optional, free). Only if you link with a coach using an invite code and approve it: your coach can see your food log, calorie/macro targets, saved meals and workouts, and can send notes and suggested targets. They never see your weight, body measurements, Apple Health data or health-related settings. You can see who has access and an access log, and revoke at any time. Coaching is a wellness tool, not a medical record, and is not a HIPAA-covered service.

No ads, no tracking, no selling of data. Purchases are handled by Apple.

Deleting data. In the app: Me → Account → Delete account removes your cloud data, coaching links and your sign-in. We keep only a one-way hash of your email and the date a free trial started, so the trial can't be restarted.

Not medical advice. Contact: support page.
