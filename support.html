FAQ

Is there a subscription? No. The app is free. Pro is a one-time purchase, with an optional free 7-day trial that never charges you automatically.

Restore Purchases: Me → Account → Restore Purchases.

Back up data: Me → Data → Export backup, or sign in for cloud sync (Pro).
